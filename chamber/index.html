Current Events in Idaho Falls!

Timmy in the Well

Timmy Sallicker has been finally found in the old Riverton Well. Unfortunately, the Fire Department currently does not have a long enough ladder reach down to where Timmy is, and Timmy Sallicker can't climb a rope, nor tie it around himself. Timmy's parents, Jim and Kerry Sallicker, are asking for donations to help buy a new ladder so the Fire Department can rescue their son GoFundMe

Found missing Credit Card

A Credit card has been found in the downtown area. The name on the back has been mostly scratched off, so if you are looking for a credit card with the numbers 1980 3449 8724 0129 with an expiration date of 9/28 and a CVV of 458, please talk to the staff at the Sandwich with a Pretty Big Pickle Restaurant

Christmas Story Come True

A story that seems to fit in better with a christmas movie rather than Idaho Falls, the Johnson Family is experiencing firsthand the meaning of the song "Grandma got ran over by a Reindeer" Mrs Johnson, an 87 year old woman, was found off the side of highway 91 with hoof marks over her body. While alive and brought to the nearest hospital, Doctors are doubtful she will make a recovery.

Not Cannibal Bakery

History in the making! Not Cannibal Bakery has been visitied by Guinness Book of World Records and awarded the world record for the most health inspection visits in one year. When asked about this achievement, the owner responded with "I do not know why we keep getting inspections. Every day, we get more inspections. I can't deal with Health Inspectors and Police every day, it's bad for buisness. But yes, we are very proud of the record."

Toy Store

The Toy Store found on downtown Walsh Street, is offering a free toy to any Child who comes in from December 1st to December 25th without parent supervision. Truly a Santa Claus story, Toy Store is sure to bring the Christmas Spriit to many underprivilaged Young Children.
